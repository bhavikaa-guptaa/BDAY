For my manakakaka

Twenty-five years of you being my big sister, and I still don't know how you make it look this easy — going first at everything so I always know it'll be okay by the time it's my turn.

I still think about us dolling up in mumma's heels and earrings, wobbling around the house playing kitchen kitchen and doctor doctor when we were SO tiny. We had no idea what we were doing and we were so sure we did. That's just proof there's no version of my life that makes sense without you leading the way in it.

So here's to 25, from your favorite (only, but still favorite) little sibling. May this year be loud, a little ridiculous, and exactly as good as you deserve.

— your little sister, always
